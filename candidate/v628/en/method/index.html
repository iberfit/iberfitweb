The IBERFIT Method

A method for better training decisions.

A professional framework that reduces improvisation without reducing people to numbers.

Every decision should be explainable, recorded and open to review.

Explore the IRI AssessmentTalk to IBERFIT

The method remains consistent across in-person, hybrid and online training.

From method to practice

Structure is there to support you, not to box you in.

Each session starts with a clear purpose, uses the information that matters and creates a basis for the next decision.

Preparing the session and its follow-up is part of the service standard.

Working cycle

Review does not happen only at the end. It is part of every cycle.

01

Assess

Understand the baseline and relevant constraints.

02

Plan

Define priorities, workload and progression.

03

Train

Train with clear instructions and practical technique.

04

Monitor

Record what is needed to interpret the response.

05

Review

Compare progress, adherence and context.

06

Adjust

Change the plan when there is a reason.

Operating principles

Structure without rigidity. Adaptation without improvisation.

Assess before planning

Individualisation begins with information—not a coach’s preference.

Adapt before standardising

Structure protects quality; content responds to the individual.

Measure before deciding

Only information that can support interpretation or adjustment is recorded.

Review before repeating

A plan is not repeated by default when the context has changed.

Visible decisions

The method is visible in what changes—and what remains consistent.

SignalInterpretationDecision
Workload is well tolerated

Technique remains consistent and recovery supports the plan.

Progression can move forward gradually.

Pain or a limitation appears

Execution no longer matches the intended plan.

The exercise is paused, adapted or replaced and the reason is recorded.

The week becomes unrealistic

Availability or recovery changes.

The workload is reorganised to protect continuity and quality.

Continuity

Quality should not depend on remembering what happened last week.

Useful recordsSessions, responses and adjustments remain connected.
