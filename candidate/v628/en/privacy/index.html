Summary

Proportionate use of data.

Contact

When you open WhatsApp or email, information is transmitted directly through those services. IBERFIT receives nothing from this website until you choose to send it.

Short format guide

The guide does not request your name, phone number or email. Selections are processed in your browser and are only added to a message if you choose to open WhatsApp. With consent, analytics may record step progression and a broad training-format or coverage category; it does not receive the full content of your answers.

Audience measurement

With your permission, Google Analytics 4 may record the page, language, device category, reading depth, engaged time and clicks on key actions. We do not send form text, messages, ID numbers, phone numbers, email addresses or clinical information.

Advertising measurement

Meta Pixel loads only if you expressly accept advertising measurement. It is used to understand general conversions, such as opening a contact channel, and does not receive message content.

Control and retention

You can accept all measurement, accept audience measurement only or keep necessary storage only. Preferences are stored in your browser and can be changed from the footer.
