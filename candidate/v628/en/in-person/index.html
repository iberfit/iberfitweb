Direct answers

In-person training: direct answers.

Direct supervision is useful when it fits the goal, the environment and a sustainable training frequency.

What does in-person training include?

It includes direct supervision during the session within an individual plan. What is observed and recorded in the session informs later decisions.

Where can sessions take place?

In an agreed suitable environment, subject to coverage. This may be at home, in a building gym or outdoors when the conditions support a good-quality session.

Who is it usually best suited to?

People who value immediate correction, shared-space support or need more direct supervision. The final recommendation depends on the starting point and context.

Is there follow-up beyond the session?

Yes. Each session sits inside the same planning and review process, so useful information carries into progression and later adjustments.

These answers summarise the service described on this page. Operational detail and the final recommendation depend on each person’s context.
