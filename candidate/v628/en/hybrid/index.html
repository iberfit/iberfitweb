Before you decide

What if I cannot do everything in person?

Hybrid coaching is designed precisely to combine direct support and independence without turning them into two separate plans.

What is hybrid personal training?

It combines selected in-person supervision with guided remote work inside one plan. They are not two separate programmes.

When can hybrid training be useful?

When direct supervision adds value but schedule, travel or autonomy make a mixed format more sustainable.

How is continuity maintained?

Planning, records and review connect in-person sessions with independent work. A different week does not mean restarting from zero.

Is remote work still reviewed?

Yes. Work completed away from the in-person session still has instructions, feedback and review within the same process.

These answers summarise the service described on this page. Operational detail and the final recommendation depend on each person’s context.
