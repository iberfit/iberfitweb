Direct answers

Online personal training at IBERFIT.

Online does not mean receiving a generic routine: it keeps individual planning, instructions, feedback and review.

What is IBERFIT online personal training?

It is an individual planning and follow-up service that does not depend on one location. The plan starts from your context and is reviewed using your response and adherence.

How is training supervised remotely?

Through clear instructions, records, feedback and process reviews. The level of supervision adapts to each person’s autonomy and needs.

Do I need specific equipment?

There is no single piece of equipment required for everyone. The plan is designed around the resources and environment available when they can support the goal safely and effectively.

Is an online plan adjusted over time?

Yes. Training response, adherence and changes in context guide when to progress, maintain, simplify or modify the plan.

These answers summarise the service described on this page. Operational detail and the final recommendation depend on each person’s context.
