Training here

When distance matters, frequency has to be intelligent.

Lo Barnechea combines urban areas, mountain-adjacent activity and very different travel conditions. The training plan has to live with that reality.

Distance and continuity

The logistical cost of an in-person session can vary dramatically by area. We decide how much presence adds value and keep the rest of the process connected instead of making every session depend on travel.

How we decide

Support does not have to disappear when every session is not in person.
