The origin of IBERFIT

A clearer way to move towards your goals.

IBERFIT grew from the experience of a Spanish personal trainer whose professional path moved through different European contexts before continuing in Chile.

He trained at university in Spain, spent a period living in Germany, returned to Spain and continued his professional experience there before moving to Chile. That journey helped shape a demanding way of working: understand before prescribing, explain decisions, observe the response and adjust when needed.

01🇪🇸Spain

University training and the first foundations of a structured approach.

02🇩🇪Germany

A period living in another European context that broadened perspective.

03🇪🇸Spain

Return to Spain and continued professional experience.

04🇨🇱Chile

The place where that journey ultimately took shape as IBERFIT.

For you, that means a service designed to understand your starting point, set clear priorities and adapt training to your goals, context and progress.

Rather than receiving a generic routine, you have a clear direction, understand what you are working on and can adjust the process when needed.
