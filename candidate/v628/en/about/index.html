Our story

Before it became a method, IBERFIT began with a question: how can we understand better in order to support better?

IBERFIT does not begin with a routine. It begins with a question that has followed the brand throughout its journey: how can training make more sense for the person actually living it?

That question grew into an ongoing interest in health, movement and training. Specialised university education in Spain provided a strong foundation. A European journey through Spanish and German contexts broadened the perspective, revealing different ways of understanding precision, consistency, process and support. Back in Spain, those ideas were applied, tested and allowed to mature. Later, in Chile, that entire journey adapted to a new reality and took on a form of its own.

A conviction

A method is only worthwhile if it helps a real person understand, decide and move forward better.

The journey adapts to a new reality and becomes IBERFIT: a distinct brand bringing together method, closeness and ongoing review.

That evolution gave rise to the IRI Assessment, ongoing review and in-person, hybrid and online training. Not as isolated products, but as different answers to the same question: what does this person need, today, to move forward with purpose?

IBERFIT continues to grow from the same idea: measure when it brings clarity, use technology when it helps, change when reality changes, and always preserve what should never be lost — judgement, conversation and humanity.

If you want to know us better

Why we work this way.

IBERFIT grew from a simple concern: training should make sense for the person in front of us, not only on paper.

What is IBERFIT?

IBERFIT is a personal training brand that turns assessment and follow-up into practical training decisions.

Where does IBERFIT come from?

It grew from a passion for training and a lasting curiosity about health, supported by specialised university education in Spain and enriched by a European journey through Spanish and German contexts before taking shape in Chile. That combination is reflected in method, precision, process and adaptation.

What makes IBERFIT different from a standard routine?

The plan starts with the person’s baseline and context rather than a template. It is then reviewed and adjusted using useful information from the process.

What role does technology play?

Technology organises information and supports follow-up; it does not replace professional decision-making. Observation, explanation and adjustment remain human.
