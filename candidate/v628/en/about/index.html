The origin of IBERFIT

A clearer way to move towards your goals.

IBERFIT starts from a simple question: why do so many people train without knowing whether what they are doing truly makes sense for them?

The brand grows from university training in Spain and a European journey that brought direct exposure to both Spanish and German contexts before taking shape in Chile. That contrast helped define a distinct way of working: assess before prescribing, plan with intent, monitor the response and adjust with judgement.

The IRI Assessment, ongoing review and different training formats grew from practical needs: understand the starting point better, connect what happens between sessions and support clients without depending on one place.

IBERFIT continues to grow by the same rule: if something does not help people understand, decide or sustain the process more effectively, it does not earn its place.

Direct answers

What IBERFIT is and where it comes from.

The brand is best understood through its working principles rather than a personal biography.

What is IBERFIT?

IBERFIT is a personal training brand that turns assessment and follow-up into practical training decisions.

Where does IBERFIT come from?

It grows from university education in Spain and a European path shaped by Spanish and German contexts before consolidating in Chile. That journey informs method, precision, process and adaptation.

What makes IBERFIT different from a standard routine?

The plan starts with the person’s baseline and context rather than a template. It is then reviewed and adjusted using useful information from the process.

What role does technology play?

Technology organises information and supports follow-up; it does not replace professional decision-making. Observation, explanation and adjustment remain human.

These answers summarise the service described on this page. Operational detail and the final recommendation depend on each person’s context.
