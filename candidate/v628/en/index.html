Questions before you start

The essentials, without the jargon.

What we do, what the IRI is for and how support changes around your real life.

What is IBERFIT?

IBERFIT is a personal training service built around assessment, planning, monitoring and ongoing review. The aim is for each training decision to have a clear reason and to adapt as the person progresses.

What is the IRI Assessment?

It is IBERFIT’s initial assessment and baseline. It organises context, current capacity and priorities before planning; it is separate from the ongoing follow-up that happens during training.

How is the plan adapted?

The plan is reviewed using training response, adherence and context. It changes when that information supports an adjustment, rather than following a fixed routine.

What is the difference between in-person, hybrid and online?

The format changes how support is delivered, not the working logic. In-person prioritises direct supervision; hybrid combines supervision and autonomy; online keeps planning, feedback and review without relying on one location.
