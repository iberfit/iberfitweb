What you receive

The assessment ends with a recommendation—not a list of results.

The IRI Report organises findings, identifies priorities and explains the next step. Its content adapts to each person.

  • Initial reading of context and current capacity.
  • Clear, reasoned priorities.
  • Recommended format and level of support.
  • A baseline for reviewing progress and making adjustments.

Judgement in practice

Measurement only matters when it changes a decision.

Recovery drops
→

Weekly volume, workload or distribution is reviewed before repeating the same plan.

Execution is consistent
→

Progression can move forward while protecting technique and tolerance.

Availability changes
→

Sessions are redistributed to protect continuity rather than punish adherence.
