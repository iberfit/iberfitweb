Training here

In a large commune, consistency starts by removing avoidable friction.

A good plan on paper can fail if every session demands difficult logistics. Area, schedule and training space are part of the decision from the start.

Schedule, access and travel

In Las Condes, “in person” is not enough information. We review travel time, access to the training space and the frequency that can realistically be repeated without turning every session into another race against the clock.

How we decide

We make training fit the week you actually have.

01

Your real week

We look at when you can genuinely train and how much travel the schedule can absorb.

02

Your goal

The goal sets priorities, but also the frequency and time commitment that are realistic.

03

Where you can train

If home or a building gym gives us a useful environment, we use it rather than adding unnecessary complexity.

04

How much supervision helps

Not everything needs to be in person. Direct supervision is reserved for the moments where it adds value.

Before booking

We first check whether we can deliver the service well, not only whether we can travel there.

Share your area, schedule and available training space. If a high in-person frequency is not the best fit, we will say so and suggest a more sustainable option.

Does your week change frequently?

Hybrid or online training can protect continuity without forcing you to reorganise the entire week around appointments.
